Policies & Manifesto

Building a vision for Limpopo through leadership, accountability and community development.

A Limpopo Built With Its People

The Limpopo Independent Movement Alliance believes that meaningful development begins with listening to communities, understanding local challenges and creating practical solutions that improve everyday life.

This manifesto provides a framework for the principles, priorities and areas of development that LIMA seeks to advance through community participation, accountable leadership and sustainable development.

01

Our Core Principles

Principles that guide our approach to leadership, participation and development.

People First

Communities should have meaningful opportunities to participate in decisions that affect their lives, neighbourhoods and future.

Accountable Leadership

Leadership should be guided by responsibility, transparency, ethical conduct and accountability to the communities being served.

Opportunity & Innovation

Limpopo's people should be supported to create, innovate, build businesses, develop skills and participate meaningfully in the economy.

Sustainable Development

Development should respond to present needs while protecting resources, the environment and opportunities for future generations.

02

Our Policy Priorities

Areas requiring sustained attention, community participation and practical action.

Economic Development & Employment

Promote an environment where local businesses, entrepreneurs, cooperatives and emerging enterprises can participate in economic activity and create sustainable opportunities.

  • Support local entrepreneurship and enterprise development
  • Encourage skills development and work opportunities
  • Promote local participation in economic activity
  • Support initiatives that strengthen township and rural economies

Education, Skills & Youth

Education and skills development are important foundations for expanding opportunity and preparing young people for employment, entrepreneurship and participation in society.

  • Promote access to skills development opportunities
  • Encourage digital literacy and technological skills
  • Support youth development initiatives
  • Connect young people with available opportunities

Infrastructure & Service Delivery

Communities require reliable infrastructure and public services that support safe, productive and dignified living conditions.

  • Support improved community infrastructure
  • Promote responsible maintenance of public facilities
  • Encourage transparent monitoring of service delivery
  • Strengthen community participation in identifying local priorities

Health & Community Wellbeing

Communities should be able to participate in initiatives that support health, safety, dignity and overall wellbeing.

  • Support community health awareness initiatives
  • Promote healthy and active communities
  • Encourage attention to vulnerable communities
  • Support partnerships that improve community wellbeing

Agriculture & Rural Development

Agriculture remains an important part of the economic and social life of many communities in Limpopo. Rural development should create pathways for productive participation and local growth.

  • Encourage agricultural entrepreneurship
  • Support small-scale and emerging producers
  • Promote skills and knowledge sharing
  • Encourage local value creation and market participation

Digital Access & Innovation

Digital technology can expand access to information, education, business opportunities and public participation.

  • Promote digital literacy
  • Encourage access to digital opportunities
  • Support technology-driven entrepreneurship
  • Encourage innovation in community development

Environment & Sustainable Development

Development and environmental responsibility should be considered together so that Limpopo's natural resources remain valuable for future generations.

  • Encourage responsible environmental practices
  • Promote community awareness and participation
  • Support sustainable local development
  • Encourage responsible use of natural resources

Good Governance & Transparency

Public institutions and leaders should remain accountable to the people. Transparency, responsible administration and meaningful public participation are essential to building trust.

  • Promote transparent communication
  • Encourage meaningful public participation
  • Support responsible use of public resources
  • Strengthen accountability mechanisms

Our Commitment

LIMA's vision is centred on a Limpopo where communities are not merely consulted, but are encouraged to participate in shaping the development of their own communities.

We believe leadership should be measured through responsibility, transparency, participation and the pursuit of practical solutions.

Building a stronger Limpopo through independent leadership, community participation and sustainable development.

About LIMA

Limpopo Independent Movement Alliance

Limpopo Independent Movement Alliance (LIMA) is an organisation committed to creating a platform for community participation, leadership and development in Limpopo.

LIMA aims to connect with communities through effective communication, transparent engagement and initiatives that address the needs and aspirations of the people.
